# Instructions on Jupyter Notebooks in Google Colab

## Requirements

- Google account

## Import Jupyter Notebooks

- Download the Jupyter Notebooks from Ilias (.ipynb files)
- Visit [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/)
- Sign in with your Google account
- Upload the notebook with `File` > `Upload notebook` > `Upload` > `Browse`
- Navigate to the folder where you stored the Jupyter Notebooks and select one

## Export Jupyter Notebooks

- The notebooks are automatically saved in your [Google
  Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/my-drive)
- They can be downloaded from Google Colab with `File` > `Download` > `Download
  .ipynb`

## Load .csv Files

- First, upload a Jupyter Notebook and run the first cell
- On the left panel, click the bottom icon called `Files`
- Click the left icon on the top row `File Upload`
- Select the .csv file and open it
